# SubPulse — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-18 · Developer: Appia Labs · Contact: subpulse@appialabs.com_

SubPulse is a local-first subscription tracker. **We do not run a server that stores your data, there is no account, no advertising and no analytics SDK.**

## What stays on your phone
Everything you enter or the app computes is stored only on your device: your subscriptions, amounts, dates, notes, custom icons, charge history, settings and the optional "where did you hear about us" answer. Backups are files you create yourself and save where you choose (a folder such as Google Drive, or the share sheet). They are optionally encrypted with a password you set (AES-256-GCM). **If you lose that password we cannot recover the backup.**

## What leaves your phone
Only the minimum needed for a feature, and never your subscription data:

| Feature | What is sent | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| Purchases (SubPulse Pro) | Anonymous purchase and entitlement information handled by Google Play / App Store and RevenueCat | RevenueCat, Inc. |
| Live currency rates (Pro) | A request for public reference rates (no personal data) | Frankfurter (ECB reference rates) |
| "Web" icons | The web address you typed, to download that site's icon | That website, or a public favicon service |
| Catalog services without a bundled logo | The official web address of the service you picked, to download its icon once and store it on your phone | That service's website, or a public favicon service |
| Catalog updates | A request for a static public file | Appia Labs static host |
| "Request a logo" | An email you send yourself (you see and approve it) | subpulse@appialabs.com |

## Permissions
- **Notifications** — local reminders scheduled on your phone. Nothing is pushed from a server.
- **Camera / photos** — only when you pick a photo as an icon; it is cropped and stored on the phone.
- **Internet** — for the features in the table above.

SubPulse does not request exact-alarm, contacts, location or "query all packages" permissions.

## Children
SubPulse is not directed to children under 13.

## Your control
Delete everything at any time with **Settings → Delete all data**, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is no server-side copy to delete.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new date appears above and the update ships with the app.
